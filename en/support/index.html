Support

For problems, suggestions or a word we got wrong, email bilicisoftwaree@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Including your device model, the app version (bottom of the Settings screen) and the level where it happened helps us help you faster.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game works fully offline. When you’re online, your progress is backed up quietly.

A word I found wasn’t accepted.

Each level accepts only words from our dictionary, and some valid words count as “bonus words”. Tell us about any word you think is missing or wrong; we update the dictionary regularly.

I changed phones. What happens to my progress?

Your progress is backed up under an anonymous ID, no sign-up needed. On your new device, open the game with the same store account and tap “Restore purchases” in the Shop to get your purchases back. If your progress doesn’t carry over, email us.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Tap “Restore purchases” in the Shop. If that doesn’t help, email us with the order number from your receipt.

How do I cancel Zinglo Pro?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google process payments, so refunds follow their policies: on iPhone use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use your Google Play order history.
