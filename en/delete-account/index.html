Delete account & data

You can delete your Zinglo Words data at any time, free of charge.

In the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Zinglo Words and go to Settings.
  2. Under “Legal & support”, tap “Delete account & data”.
  3. Confirm with “Delete permanently”. Your anonymous account and backup on our servers are deleted immediately, together with the progress on your device. An internet connection is required.

By email

If you can’t access the Game, email bilicisoftwaree@gmail.com with the subject “Zinglo Words data deletion request”. If possible, include your invite code (on the Profile screen) so we can find your account. We complete requests within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: anonymous user ID, game progress, coins and hints, stamps, player name and avatar, league and leaderboard entries, invite code.

May be kept: records we must keep by law, for the required period. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google. Anonymous usage statistics can’t be linked to you.

Have a subscription? Deleting your data does not cancel it. Cancel separately in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Permanent purchases (such as “Remove ads”) can be recovered with “Restore purchases”.
