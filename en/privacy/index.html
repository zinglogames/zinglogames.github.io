Privacy Policy

Effective: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use the Zinglo Words mobile game (the “Game”), under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and other applicable laws.

1. Data controller

Veysel Bilici (VB Tech Labs)
Contact: bilicisoftwaree@gmail.com

2. In short

  • You play without signing up; we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is kept on your device first and backed up under an anonymous ID.
  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We never see your payment details; Apple or Google handles payments.
  • If ads are shown, personalized ads are used only with your permission.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

Backups are stored, encrypted in transit and at rest, on servers of our provider Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Transfers outside your country rely on standard contractual clauses and appropriate safeguards.

Service providers we use:

  • Supabase: database and anonymous authentication (EU).
  • Apple and Google: app stores and payments.
  • RevenueCat: purchase and subscription verification.
  • PostHog: anonymous usage analytics.
  • Sentry: error and crash reporting.
  • Ad networks (Google AdMob and/or AppLovin), only when ads are shown.

We don’t share your data with any other third party except when required by law.

5. Ads and tracking

On iPhone, if tracking across other companies’ apps and websites is needed, we first ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, your advertising ID is not used. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your phone settings. In the EU and UK we ask for your consent before personalized ads. You can remove ads by buying “Remove ads” or Zinglo Pro.

6. Retention

We keep your data until you delete it. If you don’t open the Game for 24 months, your anonymous backup may be deleted. After a deletion request, data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days. Records we must keep by law are kept for the required period.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Users in Turkey have the rights listed in Article 11 of KVKK. California residents may request access and deletion; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without consent.

Send requests to bilicisoftwaree@gmail.com. We respond free of charge within 30 days. To delete your data, see Delete account & data.

8. Children

The Game is designed for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Access to server data is restricted by row-level security rules so each user can access only their own data. Connections are encrypted (TLS).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll announce significant changes in the Game; the current version is always on this page. The Turkish version of this policy is available here.
